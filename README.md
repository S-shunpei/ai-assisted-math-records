# AI-assisted mathematics research records — Shunpei Shimizu

This repository is an index of the research records that Shunpei Shimizu has published on Zenodo. They are records of personal, independent (hobby) research in which AI was used for exploration, for drafting proofs and proof candidates, for computations and their checks, and for writing. The records published from 20 October 2026 on (Japan time) were prepared mainly with Claude (Anthropic). The records of 10–11 October 2026 come from an earlier, automated phase, and several later records are new versions of, or grew out of, earlier work; this earlier work was done mainly with other AI systems.

**None of these records has been peer reviewed by human experts.** The records published from 20 October 2026 on (Japan time) were checked before publication by an internal review carried out by AI agents separate from those that did the research, including independent re-derivations and re-implementations of computations where applicable. For the new versions of the two OEIS parity records (A380558 and A380678), this review covered mainly the text added in those versions. This is not a substitute for review by human experts.

Comments and corrections are welcome through the Issues tab of this repository.

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

## Correction: earlier proofs by others (16 November 2026)

Two of the records below are **not** the first proofs of their results; we learned this on 16 November 2026. Both were found without knowledge of these earlier proofs. Other records whose results, or parts of them, had appeared earlier are marked in the Notes column of the table below.

- **No 8-regular K3-irregular graph** ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.23525881](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23525881), made public on Zenodo on 24 October 2026, Japan time). The non-existence of an 8-regular K3-irregular (triangle-distinct) graph had already been proved by A. Hak, S. Kozerenko and A. Serdiuk, *There is no 8-regular K3-irregular graph*, [arXiv:2609.37978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37978) (v1 29 September 2026), by an analysis of triangle degrees combined with integer linear programming (their code: [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23402145](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23402145)). Together with Zhang's construction for r ≥ 9 ([arXiv:2610.08478](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08478)), their paper states that an r-regular K3-irregular graph exists exactly when r ≥ 9. The Zenodo record gives an independent, later, computer-assisted proof by a different method (SAT encodings whose unsatisfiability is certified by DRAT proofs). Version 0.1.2 of the record ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.24279915](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24279915), 16 November 2026) states this in the manuscript as well.
- **The Welch function is not third-order sum-free for odd n ≥ 7** ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.23547575](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23547575), made public on Zenodo on 24 October 2026, Japan time). Conjecture 5.10 of X.-D. Hou and S. Zhao ([arXiv:2609.31489](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31489)) had already been proved by K. Cheng and J. Hu, *Counting subspaces with prescribed Welch sums*, [arXiv:2610.15694](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15694) (11 October 2026). The Zenodo record gives an independent, later proof. Version 0.1.2 of the record ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.24279988](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24279988), 16 November 2026), retitled "An independent proof that the Welch function on F_(2^n) is not third-order sum-free for odd n >= 7", states this in the manuscript as well and notes that the count of zero-sum subspaces given in the record can also be obtained from the results of Cheng and Hu.

## Records on named open questions

1. **Jaffe's Conjecture 7.2 (Frobenius problem with relatively prime multipliers).** For relatively prime a, b, let g(a, b) be the largest integer that is not of the form ax + by with x, y relatively prime non-negative integers, and let f(a, b) = ab − a − b. G. N. Jaffe ([arXiv:2610.17267](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17267)) conjectured that g(a, b) ≥ 4f(a, b) for all relatively prime a, b ≥ 2. The record gives a proof; it is computer-assisted for 279 pairs with max(a, b) < 110, and the constant 4 is best possible. [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23480212](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23480212) (v0.1.1, 20 October 2026).
2. **Togni's Conjecture 1 for k = 3 (multi-label radio labelings of paths).** O. Togni ([arXiv:2610.25863](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25863)) conjectured that the minimum span of (m, k)-radio labelings of the path P_n is ⌈(k+1)²/2⌉m − 1 for all sufficiently large n. The record proves the lower bound λ₃ᵐ(P_n) ≥ 8m − 1 for every m ≥ 1 and n ≥ 9216m² + 320m, which with Togni's upper bound gives the case k = 3. The case m = 1 was known before; the new part is m ≥ 2. [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23521039](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23521039) (v0.1.1, 21 October 2026).
3. **Cyclic Hamilton decompositions of carousel tournaments of order pq.** H. Liao, Y. Peng, G. Li and Y. Ma ([arXiv:2610.07102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07102)) proved that the carousel tournament T_pq has a cyclic Hamilton decomposition for primes 7 ≤ p < q. The record shows that for odd primes p < q, T_pq has a cyclic Hamilton decomposition if and only if (p, q) is neither (3, 5) nor (3, 7) (for p ≥ 7 this is the theorem of Liao, Peng, Li and Ma, used without re-checking its proof). Parts of the proof are computer-assisted. [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23481559](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23481559) (v0.1.1, 20 October 2026).
4. **No 8-regular K3-irregular graph** — independent later proof; see the correction above. [doi:10.5281/zenodo.24279915](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24279915) (v0.1.2, 16 November 2026).
5. **Welch function, Hou–Zhao Conjecture 5.10** — independent later proof; see the correction above. [doi:10.5281/zenodo.24279988](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24279988) (v0.1.2, 16 November 2026).
